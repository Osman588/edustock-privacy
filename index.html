Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 2026
This policy applies to the EduStock mobile application for Android.

1. Who We Are

EduStock is an independent educational application built for students in Grades 6 to 12. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body, provincial textbook board, or ministry of education. The app is developed and operated by an individual developer based in Pakistan.

When this policy says "we", "us", or "our", it refers to the developer of EduStock. When it says "you", it refers to anyone who uses the app.

2. What Information We Collect and Why

2.1 Information You Give Us Directly

Name and email address — collected when you create an account. Stored in Google Firebase. Used to identify your account and restore your progress across devices.

Phone number — collected only if you choose phone-based login. Used by Firebase for verification only. Not stored separately by us.

Profile picture — uploaded optionally by you. Stored on Google Firebase Storage. Displayed only to you inside the app.

2.2 Information Generated by Your Use of the App

Learning progress — quiz results, badges earned, topics viewed. Stored in your Firestore account document. Used to power the streak system and badge awards.

Last open date and daily streak count — recorded each time you open the app. Stored in your Firestore account document. Used only for the learning streak feature.

Language preference and theme preference — saved to your Firestore document so your settings persist across devices.

Highlights and underlines on notes — saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server.

FCM token — a device-specific token generated by Firebase Cloud Messaging. Stored in your Firestore document and linked to your account solely to route push notifications to your device. It does not identify you outside of the app.

2.3 Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

The app uses Google services that collect certain data automatically. We do not control this collection — it is described in Google's own privacy policies linked in Section 5.

Firebase Crashlytics — if the app crashes, it sends a report to Google containing the crash location in code, device model, Android version, and app version. No personal information is included in crash reports.

Google AdMob — serves advertisements to users who are not on ad-free accounts. AdMob collects your device's Google Advertising ID (a resettable identifier), device model, Android version, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This is used to decide which ads to show you and is handled entirely by Google's systems.

Firebase App Check — verifies that requests to our backend come from the genuine EduStock app. Does not collect personal information.

2.4 What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, camera data, microphone input, health information, financial information, or any data not described above.

6. Advertisements

EduStock is free to use and is supported by advertisements delivered by Google AdMob. Ads are shown only to users whose accounts have ads enabled. The app administrator can grant individual users an ad-free experience, in which case no advertisements are shown to that user.

All advertisements are restricted to family-safe, G-rated content suitable for all ages. We do not control which specific ads Google shows — ad content is determined entirely by Google's systems. You can manage ad personalisation and reset your Advertising ID through your device's Google account settings.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's app notification settings.

7. Children and Family Use

EduStock is designed for all ages. The app currently serves students in Grades 6 to 12, including users as young as 11 years old, and is rated Everyone on Google Play. The app is configured as a mixed-audience application in accordance with Google's Families policy.

All advertisements are restricted to G-rated, family-safe content. Google AdMob applies appropriate child privacy protections based on each user's context. We do not collect any personal data beyond what is described in this policy.

If a parent or guardian wishes to have their child's account removed, please contact us at the address in Section 13 and we will delete the account and its associated data promptly.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers, marketing companies, data brokers, or any organisation outside of the Google services listed in Section 5.

Your data may be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal order, and only to the extent specifically required.

9. Data Retention

Your account data (name, email, progress, preferences, badges) is retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data and Firebase Authentication account are permanently removed.

Crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics are retained for 90 days, after which Google deletes them automatically.

Local data (highlights) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage.

AdMob data is retained according to Google's own retention policies, which we do not control.

10. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time by going to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. You will be asked to confirm before deletion proceeds. This action cannot be undone.

You may also request a copy of your personal data by contacting us at the address in Section 13.

For data held by Google's systems (Crashlytics, AdMob), you can manage it through Google's account controls at myaccount.google.com.

11. Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. If you become aware of a security concern in the app, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top and notify users through the app at least 7 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after a change is posted means you accept the updated policy.
